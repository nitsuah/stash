- **motor-pool / axios — scope of assessment.** The two findings listed above (CVE-2026-42035 and CVE-2026-44495) have a listed fixed version of 1.16.1; the lockfile resolves axios to 1.17.0. This is not a blanket statement that axios 1.17.0 is free of advisories. CVE-2026-101903 affects versions below 1.20.0, but the inspected `tools/content-gen/server.js` does not call `fromDataURI` or use data-URI/form-data conversion; its axios call sites are ordinary `get`/`post` requests to the configured MoneyPrinterTurbo API. CVE-2026-101907 is specific to the fetch adapter; the service does not explicitly configure or select that adapter. These newer advisories are excluded from the finding count based on the inspected call paths and adapter configuration, not from the lockfile version alone. Reassess if those paths or adapter settings change.
