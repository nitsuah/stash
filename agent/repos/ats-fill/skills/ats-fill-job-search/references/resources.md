# Resources

Prefer primary and government sources. Links were checked on 2026-09-30; if one moves, search the publisher's site for the page title. Several are US-specific. For other countries, look for the national public employment service.

## ats-fill

- [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ats-fill/amofaeopfmaicbiijgjaojenedkkadmn)
- [Website and demo video](https://nitsuah.github.io/ats-fill/)
- [Source code (MIT)](https://github.com/nitsuah/ats-fill) · [Features](https://github.com/nitsuah/ats-fill/blob/main/docs/FEATURES.md) · [Privacy](https://github.com/nitsuah/ats-fill/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)
- [Free Gemini API key](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) (optional; enables resume parsing, answer drafting, JD tools, Interview Prep)

## Career exploration and labor-market data

- [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org/): tasks, skills, related titles, outlook for ~900 occupations
- [My Next Move](https://www.mynextmove.org/): plain-language occupation search and interest profiler
- [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/): what the job is, pay, outlook, how to get in
- [BLS: How to find a job](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/how-to-find-a-job/home.htm)
- [CareerOneStop](https://www.careeronestop.org/): US Department of Labor career toolkit

## Pay research

- [CareerOneStop Salary Finder](https://www.careeronestop.org/Toolkit/Wages/find-salary.aspx): local wages by occupation
- [BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/oes/)
- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/): crowdsourced tech compensation by company and level

## Searching and networking

- [CareerOneStop: Networking](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Network/networking.aspx)
- [CareerOneStop: Informational interviews](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Network/informational-interviews.aspx)
- [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/): US federal jobs (also an ats-fill keyed search source)
- [American Job Center finder](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/service-locator.aspx): free local career services
- [FTC: Job scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams): red flags and how to report

## Resumes and applications

- [CareerOneStop: Resumes](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/resumes.aspx)
- [CareerOneStop: Cover letters](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/cover-letters.aspx)
- [CareerOneStop: References](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/references.aspx)

## Interviews

- [CareerOneStop: Job interviews](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Interview/job-interviews.aspx) and [Get ready to interview](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Interview/get-ready.aspx)
- [MIT CAPD: The STAR method](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/the-star-method-for-behavioral-interviews/)
- [Google re:Work: Structured interviewing](https://rework.withgoogle.com/intl/en/guides/a-guide-to-structured-interviewing-for-better-hiring-practices): how many employers score interviews
- [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/): coding and behavioral prep for engineers
- [Coding Interview University](https://github.com/jwasham/coding-interview-university)
- [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer)
- [EEOC: Prohibited employment practices](https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices): questions and practices US employers may not use
