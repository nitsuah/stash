## Notes

- This is about 100 ideas, not 1,000. Reaching 1,000 would mean generating many variations and filtering them, and most would overlap.
- Ideas 1–25 are the most realistic first $100 because they go straight to paying clients.
- Ideas in sections B and D depend on sales or sponsors and are slow.
- Check each platform's current rules before you publish, especially for AI-generated content and paid extensions.
- Before taking a client job or bounty, read the terms and any payment-processor limits.
