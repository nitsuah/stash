# Listing playbook

## Title (80 characters max on eBay; front-load what buyers type)

`Brand + Model/Line + Item type + Key spec (size/capacity/color) + Condition cue`

- Good: `Nintendo Switch OLED Console White 64GB HEG-001 Tested Works w/ Dock`
- Bad: `L@@K Awesome Game System RARE!!!`

No hype words ("rare", "L@@K"), and no other brand names you aren't selling
("not Dyson"), which is keyword spam.

## Description template

```
<One-line summary: what it is and why it's a good buy.>

Condition: <New / Like new / Good / Fair / For parts>. <Specific flaws, with photo numbers.>
Tested: <what you tested and how, or "untested, sold as-is">
Includes: <everything in the box / photos>. Not included: <anything a buyer might assume>.
Measurements: <L × W × H, weight, or clothing pit-to-pit / length / inseam>
Model / part #: <exact numbers from the label>

Ships within <1–2> business days, packed with care. Smoke-free home <only if true>.
```

## Photos (the biggest lever)

- 8–12 photos, natural light, plain background. Use the first photo as the thumbnail.
- Show every flaw up close. Honest flaw photos prevent "not as described" returns.
- Include labels, serial and model numbers, measuring tape and powered-on screens.

## Pricing

- **List** at the 60th–70th percentile of recent sold comps, and accept offers.
- **Floor** at the 30th percentile, or wherever net drops below what the hassle is worth.
- **Price drops:** 10% at day 14, another 10% at day 30, then relist or move platforms
  at day 60. Send offers to watchers instead of blind drops.

## The weekly "AFK" routine (about 3 hours a week for 10–20 listings)

| When | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday morning | Photograph everything new in one batch | 45 min |
| Saturday afternoon | Write listings (Claude drafts, you check) and post them | 60 min |
| Tuesday and Friday | Pack and ship (schedule a free USPS pickup) | 2 × 20 min |
| Sunday evening | Review: stale listings, offers to watchers, price drops, and logging sales in fire | 20 min |

Automate what's safe: eBay Sales Sync imports sales, platform offer-to-watcher
tools send offers, and a scheduled Sunday review prompt runs the checklist. Never
automate messages that pretend a human is replying in real time.

## Sourcing for flips (only after decluttering works)

- Free or cheap: curb alerts, "free" Marketplace listings, garage-sale last hour,
  thrift half-price days, and liquidation bins for electronics with clear tested status.
- Buy only when the **expected profit per hour** clears your floor (~$15/hr):
  `(sell-through × (comp median − fees − label) − cost) ÷ hours`, with
  sell-through from sold vs. active comps and items that move within 30 days.
  "3× your cost" is only a quick first filter; cheap items can pass it and still
  lose money after fees and labels.
- Track every buy's cost basis in the fire ledger (Item cost), so profit and taxes are right.
